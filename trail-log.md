## Trail-to-trail decisions

After filling each row, scan the deltas vs previous trail. Use this to set the mode for the **next** trail:

| What changed | Diagnosis | Next-trail mode |
|--------------|-----------|-----------------|
| EF +3 pts AND Stamina −15 pts | Both signals agree: cooking yourself | RECOVER or REST |
| EF flat, Stamina −15 pts | Time budget burning, body fine | Stay on plan |
| EF +5 pts, Stamina holding | Acute drift (heat/dehydration) — overrides | Back off, hydrate aggressively |
| Potential dropped >5 pts | Permanent fatigue logged — recover ceiling won't return today | Drop one mode lower than usual |
| Both green, RPE >8 | Numbers say push, body says no — trust body | RECOVER |

## Per-hour fuel/water target (write actuals above)

- STEADY mode: 80 g carbs · 600 mL water + 1 electrolyte tab
